# ift458-git-labs
Git and GitHub assignments for IFT 458 at Arizona State University.

Young Timmy typed `git add .` with glee,
And pushed his whole project for free.
He shared every token,
His API's broken,
And `.env` is for all eyes to see.

He panicked when main was rejected,
Ignored what the prompt had expected.
A `--force` on the line,
Wiped the team's work and mine,
And now he's completely undetected.

The morning started organized, professional, and crisp:  
`feat: initialize routing logic, fix formatting in lisp`.  
By lunch, the optimism cracked, the standards lost their shine:  
`update button colors and resolve the build at line 9`.  

At 2:00 PM, a bug broke loose inside the caching layer:  
`small hotfix for the broken state, please answer me a prayer`.  
At 3:15, despair began to subtly take control:  
`fix for previous fix that took a toll upon my soul`.  

By four o’clock, semantics died, the grammar hit the floor:  
`testing stuff`. Then three ticks later: `wip on stuff once more`.  
`why isn't this deploying?` crept across the branch's face,  
Followed closely by: `i hate this framework and this place`.  

At 5:30, sanity completely lost its hold:  
`asdfghjkl`.  
`kill me now`.  
`revert what I was told`.  

At midnight, one lone push sailed out to wrap the frantic race:  
`final final REALLY FINAL please just work for god's sake`.
